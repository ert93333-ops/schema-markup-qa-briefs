FAQ

Structured-data constraints are explicit.

Does it crawl my site?

No. This MVP only analyzes pasted JSON-LD and the visible-page summary you provide.

Does it replace Google's Rich Results Test?

No. Use this as pre-publish QA, then validate with official tools before release.

Does it guarantee rich results?

No. Structured data can make pages eligible, but Google does not guarantee rich-result appearance.

Does it support Microdata or RDFa?

The first MVP focuses on JSON-LD because it is the fastest static path for technical SEO and ecommerce teams.
